## Conceptual lineage

The project is inspired by prior YAXCHÉ research, especially the CADIPHI concept: preserving the original human request, separating facts/inferences/authorizations/restrictions, detecting material ambiguity, compiling structured intent, and refusing to treat interpretation as authorization.

Conceptual inspiration is not represented here as new invention created during the hackathon.
